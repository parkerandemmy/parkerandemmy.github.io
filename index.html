Travel & Lodging

Airports

The closest airport is the Portland International Jetport, which is just a 45-minute drive to Kamp Kohut. Boston Logan Airport is a 2.5 hour drive to Kamp Kohut. If you’re not driving from your home, you will likely need to rent a car. If you are flying and prefer not to rent a car, let the bride and groom know and we will do our best to arrange transportation with other drivers (more information on this as we get closer to the date).

Lodging

Lodging options include on-site shared cabins at Kamp Kohut, local hotels, and Airbnbs. We hope that many will stay with us in the en-suite cabins at Kamp Kohut! Cabins will be the most affordable lodging option, and all meals for the weekend will be provided for those who stay with us. A brief description of the cabins can be found in the FAQs. You will be able to sign up to stay in a cabin on your RSVP.

Ubers cannot be counted upon at Kamp Kohut as this is a relatively rural area. Anyone choosing to stay at a hotel or Airbnb should be prepared to drive themselves.

Hotels are about 6 – 8 miles away and a 15-minute drive on beautiful but unlit Maine roads. Nearby hotels include:

Frequently Asked Questions

What’s the deal with the cabins?

Kamp Kohut has 18 cabins with en-suite bathrooms right on site. These cabins are spacious with plenty of beds (linens provided). Guests who stay in the cabins will have all meals included in the price of lodging. In your RSVP, we will include a detailed description of the cabins and an option to sign up. Once we have a final headcount, we will be assigning guests to cabins (please expect to be placed with friends and family!). Additional information about the cabins will be available on this website once formal wedding invitations are sent out, including the cost of cabins and how to pay. Feel free to reach out to the bride and groom with any questions.

Can we bring our children?

The ceremony and reception are child-free. We will not be providing childcare for the weekend. If you have additional questions about bringing children, please ask the bride and groom.
